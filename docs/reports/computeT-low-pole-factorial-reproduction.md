## findings

The package result for the specified input is:

| M | Modulus | Observed T(g) = 1/4 | Required T(g) = 1/2 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 9 | 7 | 5 |

The exact cohomological calculation is short. At pole five write $g=(x_0x_1^2/2)\partial_0F$. Griffiths–Dwork reduction gives

$$
\left[\frac{x_0^2x_1^2\Omega}{F^5}\right]
=\frac1{8}\left[\frac{x_1^2\Omega}{F^4}\right]
=\frac1{8}\frac1{6}\left[\frac{\Omega}{F^3}\right]
=\frac1{48}\left[\frac{\Omega}{F^3}\right].
$$

Thus the prescribed map has

$$T(g)=4!\cdot\frac1{48}=\frac12.$$

The implementation carries the factorial-scaled numerator representation. Each derivative-only reduction contributes $1/2$, leaving $1/4$ at pole $h=3$. The relation branch at pinned source line 62 weights extracted coordinates by $(h-1)!$. The low-degree branch at lines 83–94 extracts the coordinate without that weight. Here $(h-1)!=2$, accounting exactly for the observed factor-two discrepancy.

This is a normalization defect of the pinned package in the stated environment, not a working-precision shortage: $1/2-1/4=1/4$ is a 3-adic unit. Raising M cannot make the two outputs agree even modulo 3. A future repair must restore the factorial at the low-pole extraction and use appropriate exact factorial arithmetic; this report implements no repair and certifies no full driver/backend behavior.
