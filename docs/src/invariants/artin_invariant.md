# Artin invariant

Let $X$ be a supersingular K3 surface over a field of characteristic $p > 2$,
i.e. one of infinite [Artin–Mazur height](artin_mazur_height.md). Then the
Néron–Severi lattice of $X_{\bar{k}}$ has rank $22$ and discriminant
$-p^{2\sigma}$ for an integer $1 \le \sigma \le 10$. This integer $\sigma$ is
the Artin invariant of $X$.
